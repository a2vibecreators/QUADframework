>

> - Your own WhatsApp number
> - Your colors, your logo, your curriculum
> - Everything connected seamlessly!"

### Phase 4: White-Label for Customer
**Process:**
1. School signs contract ($5K setup + $500/month)

   - Admin (full control)
   - Teacher (manage students, view progress)

### Phase 5: Connect Everything
- Link to their existing LMS (if they have one)
- Payment gateway integration
- Single Sign-On (SSO)
- Email notifications
- Analytics dashboard

**Message:** "Everything works seamlessly. Your students never leave your ecosystem."

### Phase 6: Hand Off
**Training:**

>

>

> - Your customizations
> - Your data management
> - Your server uptime (if self-hosted)
> - Your support to students
>
> We ARE responsible for:
> - Updates to SQUAD base platform
> - Security patches
> - Bug fixes in core features
> - Documentation"

**Support Levels:**
- **Starter Plan ($500/month):** Email support (48hr response)
- **Pro Plan ($1,500/month):** Priority support (24hr response)
- **Enterprise Plan ($5,000+/month):** Dedicated account manager + 4hr response

### Phase 7: Release Updates
**Every 3 months:**

**Our Recommendation:**
